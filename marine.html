Electric marine mobility

A complete range of electric and hybrid marine motors — high performance combined with reduced weight and dimensions, from 1 kW auxiliaries to 120 kW planing power.

The philosophy

Taste the sound of silence.

Huracan Marine designs and produces innovative electric and hybrid marine motors for boats. The goal is simple: less pollution, cleaner seas. Every product balances high-voltage performance with minimal weight, water-cooled reliability and IP69 protection.
